My Wealth Navigation Framework

Navigate your wealth.
Build your life.

A holistic framework covering the financial decisions that shape your life, your family and the legacy you leave behind.

01 — FINANCIAL CLARITY

Understand

Understand your current financial position—your income, expenses, assets, liabilities, protection and investments.

02 — PROTECTION PLANNING

Protect

Protect your income, health, family and financial goals against life's unexpected events.

03 — INVESTMENT PLANNING

Grow

Create an investment strategy aligned with your goals, time horizon and risk profile.

04 — RETIREMENT PLANNING

Prepare

Work backwards from the lifestyle you want and determine what you need to build towards financial independence.

05 — ESTATE PLANNING

Pass It On

Make sure the wealth you've worked hard to build is transferred according to your wishes.

May include reviewing wealth structure, nominations, beneficiaries and estate distribution strategy.
06 — FAMILY & LEGACY PLANNING

Leave Meaning

Plan for the people and milestones that matter—from children's education to family responsibilities and the legacy you want to leave behind.

07 — ONGOING WEALTH MANAGEMENT

Evolve

Your life doesn't stay the same. Your income changes. Your family grows. Your priorities evolve. Your financial plan should evolve with you.

About Catherine

I'm not here to tell you what to buy.
I'm here to help you see the bigger picture.

I started my career in corporate sales before entering financial planning in 2018.

Over the years, I realised something: many people don't lack financial products. They lack clarity.

They may have an insurance policy here, an investment there, some EPF, savings, property and other assets—but no clear picture of how everything fits together.

That's where I believe a good financial planner adds value. I help you step back, look at the bigger picture, identify the gaps and make informed decisions based on what you actually want your life to look like.

“You can have all the wealth in the world, but if you don't have the health to enjoy it, what is it worth?”

Today, I also work in the movement and wellness space as a Pilates instructor. As both a Licensed Financial Planner and Pilates instructor, I see these two areas as deeply connected.

From Financial Planning to Financial Education

We can't learn about money if we don't talk about it.

Early in my financial planning career, I hosted My Personal Finances PodCat, where I had the privilege of having honest conversations about money and life with people from all walks of life.

Money can be a taboo subject, especially in Asian culture. Better financial decisions often begin with simply having the right conversation.
