The Real Problem

You don't need more financial products.

You need a clearer direction.

You may already have insurance, investments, EPF, savings and property.
But do they actually work together?

Am I financially on track?
Do I have enough protection for myself and my family?
Am I investing according to my goals?
How much do I really need for retirement?
How should I plan for my child's future?
What happens to my wealth if something happens to me?
Have I properly planned how my assets will be passed on?

Financial planning begins by answering these questions — not by recommending a product.

My Wealth Navigation Framework

Navigate Your Wealth. Build Your Life.

Seven connected pillars that turn scattered financial products into one coherent strategy.

01

Financial Clarity

Understand your current financial position — your income, expenses, assets, liabilities, protection and investments.

02

Protection Planning

Protect your income, health, family and financial goals against life's unexpected events.

03

Investment Planning

Create an investment strategy aligned with your goals, time horizon and risk profile.

04

Retirement Planning

Work backwards from the lifestyle you want and determine what you need to build towards financial independence.

05

Estate Planning

Make sure the wealth you've worked hard to build is transferred according to your wishes.

This may include reviewing your wealth structure, nominations, beneficiaries and estate distribution strategy.

06

Family & Legacy Planning

Plan for the people and milestones that matter — from children's education to family responsibilities and the legacy you want to leave behind.

07

Ongoing Wealth Management

Your life doesn't stay the same. Your income changes. Your family grows. Your priorities evolve.

Your financial plan should evolve with you.

Building wealth is only half the journey. Knowing how to pass it on matters too.

My Philosophy

Health is wealth. Wealth is choice.

I believe wealth should never be measured by money alone.

Your health allows you to enjoy your wealth.

Your wealth gives you the freedom to make choices.

And together, they give you the ability to live life with greater confidence and intention.

As both a Licensed Financial Planner and Pilates instructor, I see these two areas as deeply connected.

Strong Body. Strong Wealth. Strong Life.

Financial planning isn't simply about accumulating more. It's about having the health, resources and freedom to live the life you worked so hard to build.

About Catherine

I'm not here to tell you what to buy.

I'm here to help you see the bigger picture.

I started my career in corporate sales before entering financial planning in 2018. Over the years, I realised something: many people don't lack financial products. They lack clarity.

They may have an insurance policy here, an investment there, some EPF, savings, property and other assets — but no clear picture of how everything fits together. That's where I believe a good financial planner adds value.

I help you step back, look at the bigger picture, identify the gaps and make informed decisions based on what you actually want your life to look like.

Today, I also work in the movement and wellness space as a Pilates instructor. That experience has reinforced one of my strongest beliefs:

You can have all the wealth in the world, but if you don't have the health to enjoy it, what is it worth?
